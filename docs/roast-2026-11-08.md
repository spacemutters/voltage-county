# Roast, 2026-11-08: the city before soft launch

One fresh reviewer's verdict on the nine `docs/images/launch-polish/` shots, against `docs/city/ARCHETYPES.md`, real
Brickell / Biscayne Bay / Ocean Drive, and the best Roblox city games. Worst first.

**Verdict.** From across the bay (01) it passes for a decent mid-tier city. Look down (03) or stand in it (05) and it's
a free-model city on a green baseplate, with toy colours and grey concrete everywhere.

## The 10 worst problems

**1. The baseplate shows.** In 03, 07 and 09 the flat olive floor runs to the horizon, with cube trees (07 bottom left,
09 foreground) and the map's edge in plain view. That's the thing that most gives away a free model. Fix: tint terrain
grass to #7A9A52 with a second patchy colour #8FA866 painted in blobs, set Atmosphere to Density 0.35, Offset 0.2,
Haze 1.8, Color #CFDDE3 so the edge fades out, and put a ring of 40 low mangrove and palm clumps along the county edge.
Delete every cube tree.

**2. The Lego primaries.** The pure red triangle roof, the solid yellow square "sky court" and the yellow balcony bars
(04 centre, 02 right, 01 middle) are flat SmoothPlastic primaries. The real building's accents are muted, and its sky
court is a deep hole you can see into. Fix: red #B5483B, yellow #D9AE4A. Recess the sky court 24 studs, with a dark
back wall #2B3A40, a slab floor and one palm inside. Also tone down the orange stepped block in 01/04 to #A9653F.

**3. The water is a plastic sheet.** In 01 the bay is a flat cyan with hard bridge shadows and a pale stripe of seabed
across it. Fix: WaterColor #1E6F7C, WaterTransparency 0.35, WaterReflectance 0.7, WaveSize 0.12, WaveSpeed 10. Drop
the seabed 8 studs wherever the stripe shows, and keep shallow sand only in a 20-stud band at the shore.

**4. Dead concrete acres.** Half of 03 and the bottom 55 % of 05 are one flat grey Concrete colour. "Filled lots" still
read as empty: three umbrellas on a slab, plus a red rectangle. Fix (textures are free): put a paver Texture on every
plaza top (StudsPerTile 6, Transparency 0.3), add white stall lines on the car parks (a Decal per row), a 4x4 planter
with a shrub at the end of each stall row (about 8 per lot), and 6-10 parked car shells per lot. Give the sports
courts a 1-stud fence and a light pole at each corner.

**5. The "blinds" read as missing textures.** In 06 and 02 the pale grey rectangles look like holes in the texture,
and there are too many, too bright, at the same grey. Lit rooms in daytime add to it. Fix: blinds at #8FA3B0, no more
than 12 % of panes, and no lit rooms in daytime. Add a second Texture per face holding a vertical sky-reflection
gradient (light at the top, darker at the bottom, Transparency 0.55, StudsPerTileV = the face height) so each face
reads as one sheet of glass and not a spreadsheet.

**6. Every tower wears the same white wireframe.** In 02, 04 and 06 there are bright white mullions and slab edges on
everything, so seven different buildings look like one kit. Fix: white only where the real building is white. Use
silver #A9AFB4 or dark bronze #3B3A36 elsewhere, edges 0.6 thick and set 0.3 behind the glass.

**7. The beach is an empty sand desert.** In 01 there's a huge pale strip with one lifeguard stand and two dots.
Ocean Drive is umbrellas, chairs, palms at the dune line and pastel hotels with neon fins. Fix: 3 rows of umbrella
plus double-lounger sets every 14 studs (2 parts each, about 120 parts), 3 more lifeguard huts in different pastels,
a palm every 20 studs along the dune edge, and a sand texture with ripples.

**8. No ground floors.** In 05 and the base of 02 the towers meet the pavement with blank glass. Nothing says a shop,
lobby or café. Fix: a 14-stud storefront band on every street face, with a dark glass #24323A, an awning 3 deep in
one of 4 house colours, a SurfaceGui sign, and 2 planters. About 10 parts a building.

**9. Bald roofs.** In 03 every roof is a flat white lid. Fix: a membrane Texture on every roof, plus 3-5 parts each:
AC boxes, a stair bulkhead, a water tank, or a pool and lounger deck on the condos. Give two towers a helipad decal.

**10. Flat noon lighting.** Every face is lit the same, the sky is oversaturated, and there's no distance haze, so
the skyline has no depth. Fix: Lighting ShadowSoftness 0.15. ColorCorrection Contrast 0.1, Saturation -0.08, Tint
#FFF3E4. Bloom 0.4 / 0.95 threshold, SunRays 0.04, plus the Atmosphere from point 1.

## Towers that don't read as their inspiration

- **triple_tower_podium (Icon Brickell): no.** In 02 it's three square boxes on a 4-storey base. Fix: round each
  tower's narrow ends (Cylinder r = 20, per the spec). That's the silhouette. After that, raise the podium to 84.
- **telescope_tower (Elysee): mostly yes.** It's the only one you'd recognise (07). But the step-outs are timid.
  Fix: make each tier 8 studs wider per side instead of 5, with a dark 3-stud soffit band, so the top tier clearly
  overhangs.
- **fin_column_tower (Missoni Baia): no.** In 08 it's two flat white side walls with a saturated blue stripe down the
  middle. Fix: delete the stripe and the side walls, and stand four 4x14 blade fins diagonally at the corners, raked
  off at the top. Then shift the balcony bands 12 studs band to band for the cascade.
- **tiered_curve_crown_tower (Miami Tower): no.** In 09 it's a round wedding cake on a thin neck, sitting on an
  office block. Fix: curve only the bay face and make the back flat, with each tier setting back on the curved side
  only, so it's a stepped quarter-wheel. Rebuild the base as the K2 garage it is meant to be.

## Three things that work (don't break them)

1. The palm avenue (04). Real mesh palms in dense double rows. This is the most Miami frame in the set.
2. The telescope tower's widening silhouette and white soffits (07). It reads at a distance.
3. Varied pane tints and patterns per tower (02, 06). The idea is right; only the blinds' value and count are wrong.
