Voltage County on Roblox

Privacy

Last updated October 21, 2026

The short version

  • Your game progress is saved in Roblox's own storage, under your Roblox account.
  • We never ask for your name, email, age, photos or where you live.
  • The AI Car Designer gets only your car description, after Roblox's filter has checked it. It never gets your name, your Roblox account or your chat.
  • Design records with a player code are deleted after 90 days.
  • We don't sell anything about you, show you ads based on you, or build profiles of players.

What the game saves

Everything below is kept in Roblox's own storage (Roblox calls it data stores), linked to your Roblox player number so the game can load it when you come back.

Payments are handled by Roblox. We only learn that a purchase happened, so the game can give you the item. Chat is Roblox's own chat: the game doesn't store it, and nothing you say in chat goes to the AI. If you report a car, the report goes to that game server's log and a short list for our moderators. It isn't saved with your progress.

The AI Car Designer

When you ask for a new car or a mod, the game first runs your words through Roblox's text filter. Only what passes leaves Roblox. It goes to our small design service, which asks an AI to turn it into a car and checks the answer before the game builds it.

What is sent

  • Your description, after Roblox's filter
  • The car class you picked, or for a mod, the car as it looks now
  • A scrambled player code: a one-way code made from your player number and a secret, which the design service can't turn back into your account
  • The version of the game's car parts

What is never sent

  • Your username or display name
  • Your Roblox player number
  • Your chat, friends or anything else about your account
  • Your other cars, cash or progress

The scrambled player code stays with our design service. It only counts how many designs each player asks for, so nobody can use them all up. The AI itself receives only your description and the car class or car.

Please don't type personal information, like your real name or address, into the designer. The game reminds you before your first design.

Children and families

Voltage County is made for players of all ages. We follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Roblox's rules for children's data:

  • We don't ask for or keep contact or identity details from children or anyone else: no names, emails, phone numbers, ages, photos or locations.
  • The game uses your Roblox player number only to load your save and run the game, inside Roblox.
  • Text players type is checked by Roblox's filter before anyone else can see it, and the designer's text is checked before it leaves Roblox.
  • The scrambled player code is used only to run the designer fairly (limits per player), never for ads or profiles.
  • Ads, when the game shows them, come only through Roblox's own ad system. Roblox decides who can see an ad; we never receive or share anything about you for ads.
  • A parent can ask us to delete their child's game data at any time (see below).

Things we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade information about players.
  • Send you to websites or ask you to pay outside Roblox.
  • Send your chat, your username or your account to the AI.
  • Keep design records with a player code longer than 90 days.

Who helps run the game

  • Roblox runs the game, the saves, chat, purchases and ads. Its own privacy policy covers your Roblox account.
  • Vercel runs our small design service.
  • Supabase stores the design service's 90-day request log and the finished designs.
  • Anthropic makes the AI that designs the cars.

Each of them only gets what is listed on this page.

Deleting your data

  • If you delete your Roblox account, Roblox tells us and we delete your game data: your save, your drag strip times, the design service's records for you, and your player number on any limited serial number (the number itself stays used, so no one else gets it).
  • You, or a parent, can also ask us to delete your game data without deleting your account. Send us your Roblox username as shown below. Your progress and designs will be gone for good.

Contact us

Open Voltage County on Roblox, tap the creator's name under the game's title to open their Roblox profile, and send a message from there. A parent can do this from their own Roblox account. Please don't include personal details beyond your Roblox username.
