# Roast, 2026-10-24

One fresh reviewer's verdict on the game before launch (Davis's rule: roast everything before handing it over),
compared with Greenville, Driving Empire, Southwest Florida, Emergency Response: Liberty County, Car Dealership
Tycoon and Jailbreak. Verbatim, worst first.

**1. The "realistic" cars look like melted toys, not Teslas.** Evidence: `docs/art-pass-contracts.md:10-13, 27-31`:
each car is one AI blob body + glass/lights/4 wheels, non-uniformly stretched to the old greybox, then its texture is
deleted ("TextureID cleared") so every grille, vent and panel line is wiped to one flat colour. No interior, no opening
doors. The screenshots: `art-pass-test-ev-fastback` has a crumpled tailgate; `art-pass-test-city-car` has rippled doors
and blank white disc wheels; the yellow Ardenza in `art-pass-montevale` is covered in black blotches; `art-pass-ohm`'s
Arc, your thumbnail hero, is a red bubble with a black ball on a stalk. And knockoffs survived: the white winged Kaze
reads as a famous 90s Japanese turbo coupe, the blue Aldermoor classic as the iconic 60s British long-hood coupe, the
black Brannock as the late-60s movie muscle car. Greenville sells cars with "real life options", parts and wheels.
Fix: treat generated meshes as drafts. Hand-finish 6 hero cars first (Arc, Spark, Current, Vorrena, Ardenza,
Halberd): clean topology, separate doors/hood, a basic interior (dash, wheel, seats). Keep the surface detail in maps
and tint the paint on top instead of deleting the texture. Scale uniformly and fit the chassis to the body, not the
body to the greybox. Remake the three lookalikes.

**2. The game is silent.** Evidence: no engine or EV sound exists anywhere; the only `Instance.new("Sound")` calls
are the thruster hiss (`AbilityController.luau:774`) and light-show music (`LightShowController.luau:476`), and both
ids are 0 (`Config/Audio.luau:47, 56, 65, 77`). No horn, indicator or headlight key (`Config/Controls.luau:55-69`).
Jailbreak sells a Car Stereo pass for 200 Robux and Driving Empire a Car Radio pass. Fix: an EV whine that rises with
speed; fuel engines that rev through the existing 5-gear automatic (`Driving.luau:141-148`) and drop on each shift;
tyre squeal on slip and wind at speed; horn (H), indicators and hazards, headlights (L). Make the sounds with
ElevenLabs (allowed by `Audio.luau`'s own rules) and upload the three light-show tracks. Sell a Car Radio pass.

**3. The county is still a grey block-out.** Evidence: `county-topdown-2026-10-19.jpg` is mostly flat olive floor;
downtown is windowless coloured boxes (`art-pass-test-recolor`). No terrain or trees, no day/night (nothing in the repo
sets the time of day), so light-show meets happen at permanent noon. Traffic is 12 identical grey robotaxi pods for the
whole county, and they pass through you (`Config/Traffic.luau:22, 94-98, 129-131`). PLAN.md §5 left map art to "Davis
or a builder", and it was never done. Greenville advertises a "large detailed map"; ER:LC doubled its map to a city,
countryside and town. Fix: terrain grass and hills, 2,000+ trees, window/facade textures on every building, 5
landmarks (Ohm factory, lake bridge, water tower, stadium, a diner meet spot), a 24-minute day/night cycle with working
streetlights, and 40 traffic cars built from the real car bodies that stop for you and can be hit.

**4. The paid AI Car Designer hands you a grey block car.** Evidence: the realistic body is "session-only"; the saved
car always rebuilds from its kit spec (`Config/Designer.luau:98-100`), so on your next server you own a block car like
`looks-grid-2026-10-20.jpg`. Stats are capped at 85 % of the class range (`Config/DesignKit.json:138-139`), so every
paid design is slower than the dealership cars. Roblox's own car generator is the free core loop of other games. Fix:
store the generated body's mesh data (under 20,000 triangles fits a save slot) and rebuild it on spawn; test 10
designed cars in one server against the rebuilt-mesh memory limit; allow the full stat range. If the body can't be
kept, take the designer out of the title and description.

**5. The supercars are pointless and the price ladder is upside down.** `Config/Vehicles.luau`: Spark Performance $60k
3.0 s (148-150); a used Ohm Grand $150k 2.4 s (462-464); Corvane $360k 3.2 s (1612-1614); Valtero $420k 2.9 s
(1514-1516); Ardenza $520k 2.8 s (1712-1714). The luxury class allows down to 2.2 s (`Classes.luau:155`). Top speed is
the supercars' only edge, and the game kills it: drive pay caps at $360 a minute (`DriveEarn.luau:28-47`), and the
County Loop takes about a minute at 220 mph. At the bottom, the $18k Tovi (95 mph, 9 s, 537-539) is worse than the free
car (105 mph, 6 s, 113-114). Nothing under $35k beats your starter. Fix: supercars at 2.1-2.6 s, nothing outside the
supercar and hypercar classes under 3.0 s (Spark Performance 3.6, Grand 3.2, Mesa Performance 3.4), the starter a
beater (95 mph, 8.5 s), and top-speed boards and speed traps on the Loop.

**6. No emergency services and no crime, which is the core of the genre.** The only jobs are rideshare, delivery,
towing and freight (`Config/Jobs.luau:80`), all "drive to the marker". The rideshare passenger never gets in the car;
it fades away (`JobVisualsController.luau:5-7`). Emergency and roadside roles were pushed past launch. ER:LC is police,
sheriff, fire, EMS and road crews against criminals, and sells SWAT 750, Premium Jobs 400 and K-9 400 Robux. Fix: a
Sheriff team with radar on the Loop (speed limits already in `RoadGraph`), traffic stops and tickets as a cash sink, no
weapons; a "heat" system for street racers so there is someone to chase; fire and EMS answering crashes and the
existing breakdown spots; a passenger who actually sits in the car.

**7. Roleplay is a checklist.** A house is a solid block you can't enter (`Config/Houses.luau:206-213`) with five
upgrades worth $44,500 in total, then nothing to buy (452-485). A "car meet" is a toast when 4 cars park
(`Social.luau:36`). Default emotes, and the phone has no messages, camera or radio. Greenville advertises "RP tools,
houses"; ER:LC sells Premium Housing. Fix: enterable houses with furniture placement (a lasting cash sink), phone
texting and a photo mode, meet tools (hood up, lights, a shared radio), garages that display your cars.

**8. The first five minutes have no hook.** You spawn on foot, walk to a prompt (`SpawnFlowService.luau:4-8`), get
"Enjoy the drive!" (`UsedLot.luau:108`), then nothing. No goal, no arrow; jobs, the designer and test drives are hidden
in a phone behind P. Fix: 0:00 spawn already seated in the Spark while the Arc boost-hops over you; 0:15 a GPS line to a
fare 600 studs away that pays $3,000; 1:30 a prompt to test drive the Arc (the 2-minute test drive already exists,
`Dealership.luau:58-59`); 4:00 "Design your free car"; then a daily-goals card. Every step one tap.

**9. The store sells nothing real, and there are no events at launch.** Every "exclusive" wrap (Starter Pack, Voltage
Club, the numbered Launch Week limited, all three Season wraps) renders on catalog cars as one flat colour
(`Customization.luau:415-487`), which $3,000 of in-game custom paint matches or nearly matches (315-324). Light Show Pro
sells routines whose music is silent. The season, the launch limited and every event start on Jan 15, 2027
(`Season.luau:90`, `Limiteds.luau:78`, `Events.luau:50`), so launching now gives about 12 weeks of nothing. Greenville
sells passes by car type (Sports 500, Luxury 250, Zero Emissions 125); Driving Empire puts new limiteds in its title;
Car Dealership Tycoon is running a Halloween event right now. Fix: exclusives that paint can't copy (printed liveries,
chrome or colour-shift finishes, underglow); a Car Radio pass, a Sports/Exotic access pass and one Robux car per drop
(stats still inside its class band); Season 1 starting on launch day; a holiday event with a limited car.

**10. There is too little to chase.** 32 vehicles. A mid-level player owns the Arc at about 16 h and the Arc Boost at
about 44 h, then nothing until January. No performance tuning, so the "made to be modified" Kaze Kobi
(`Vehicles.luau:1158`) has nothing to modify. None of the 8 brands has a logo (`Brands.luau`, every `logoAssetIds`
empty). Greenville has "hundreds of vehicles"; Driving Empire has 200+. Fix: tuning (engine, tyres, weight, gearing)
inside each class band, 8-10 new cars every two weeks, brand badges on every car and dealership, and collection
rewards for owning a whole brand.

**What makes a player quit in the first session:** they load into a silent grey downtown at permanent noon, walk to a
prompt, get a used EV with no engine note, and drive empty streets where the only traffic is ghost pods that pass
through them. Nothing tells them what to do. If they find the Jobs app, the "passenger" is a block figure that
vanishes. The cars on display look melted next to Greenville's licensed ones. The Arc from the thumbnail is 16 hours
away, and the first car they can afford is slower than the free one. By minute three they have seen everything the
server does, and Greenville is one click away. On top of that, nothing in the store can be bought yet, chat is still
on the legacy system, and nobody has played it on a phone.
